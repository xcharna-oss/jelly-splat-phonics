Say just the word, close to the phone, then stop. For the sound sets say the pure sound — “sss”, not “ess”, and “t” without the “uh” on the end. Hands-free runs the whole set on its own: the word appears, it counts you in, records two seconds and moves on — put the phone down and just say each word as it comes up.

Or photograph the pages

Snap each page of the reading book. The phone reads the printed words itself, pulls out the ones worth practising and keeps the real sentences — then she can read the actual pages here. The first time you use it, it downloads the reader (about 4MB) and needs signal; after that it works offline. Check what it read before you save it — photos of small print can confuse it.
